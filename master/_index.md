# Course overview – School · Computer Science through Upper Secondary Education {#kurseinstieg}

## Who is this course for? {#S1}

This self-study course begins with everyday computing systems and moves from first programs to databases, algorithms, automata, projects and Abitur practice. You can begin with integer arithmetic and basic file skills. Python 3.11 or newer and SQLite are sufficient for the executable examples. Lessons include worked examples, teaching diagrams and exercises with explained solutions.
